How it works

From idea to build plan in one pass.

No hardware background required to start — just describe the thing you're trying to build.

  1. Write your brief

    Describe the device in plain language: what it senses or controls, how it's powered, and any constraints that matter to you.

  2. Choose your build target

    Hobbyist for breadboard-friendly modules and Arduino-style code, or Advanced for bare ICs and custom-PCB-oriented guidance.

  3. The agent designs the circuit

    It selects chips, wires them together, writes firmware, and lays out a build plan — all generated together so they stay consistent.

  4. Review, copy, build

    Check the diagram and BOM, copy the firmware, and follow the numbered steps — or refine the brief and generate again.
